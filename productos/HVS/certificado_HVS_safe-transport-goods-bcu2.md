序号 检查结果及其他事项 **No. Inspection results and other things** 

本报告所述锂电池已通过联合国《试验和标准手册》第 III 部分 38.3 小节相应测试要求（证书编号： 01052000000884-1(E), UN38.3 试验概要： 01052000000884-2(E) ）。 

- 1 Lithium cells and batteries listed in this report are of the types proven to meet the requirements of each applicable test in the UN Manual of Tests and Criteria, Part III, sub-section 38.3(Test Report No.: 01052000000884-1(E) ， UN38.3 test summary ： 01052000000884-2(E)). 

- 本报告所述锂电池按照《国际海运危险货物运输规则》（ 39-18 版） 2.9.4 （ 5 ）规定的质量管理体系进行 制造。 

- 2 Lithium cells and batteries listed in this report were manufactured under the quality management programmer as described in IMDG CODE (Amdt.39-18) 2018 Edition 2.9.4(5). 

- 电池和电池组，除安装在设备上的之外，应使用内容器包装，将电池和电池组完全包裹。应保护电池 和电池组防止发生短路。 

- 3 Cells and batteries, except when installed in equipment, shall be packed in inner packaging that completely enclose the cell or battery. Cells and batteries shall be protected so as to prevent short circuits. 

- 锂电池有适当的保护措施防止其在包装件内移位。 

- 4 Cells and batteries are properly protected so as to secure against movement within the outer package. 

- 每个包装件必须使用 9 类锂电池危险性标签 (9A 号 ) 。 

- 5 Each package must be labelled with the Class 9-Lithium Battery hazard label (No.9A). 

备注 适用于海运。 Remark Be applicable to transport by sea
