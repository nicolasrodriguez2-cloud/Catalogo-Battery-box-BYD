## **BATTERY-BOX PREMIUM LVS LITE** 

- 4 kWh Module 

- Modular Design Simplifies Transport and Installation 

The BYD Battery-Box Premium LVS Lite is a lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery pack for use with an external inverter. A single Battery-Box Premium LVS Lite contains between 1 to 4 battery modules LVS Lite stacked in parallel and can reach 4 to 16.0 kWh usable capacity in one tower: 

- Battery-Box LVS Lite 4.0 (4 kWh) 

- Battery-Box LVS Lite 8.0 (8 kWh) 

- Battery-Box LVS Lite 12.0 (12 kWh) 

- Battery-Box LVS Lite 16.0 (16 kWh) 

Connect up to 2 Battery-Box LVS Lite 16.0 in parallel for a maximum size of 32 kWh. Ability to scale by adding LVS Lite modules or parallel towers of 1 to 4 modules.
